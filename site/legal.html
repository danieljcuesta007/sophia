Privacy

Sophia for Chrome

  • Your text is checked by code running in your browser. Sophia has no account and no server, and it sends nothing you write anywhere.
  • It reads only the text you select, or the text box you're typing in, to check it.
  • Your settings (your name, theme and which checks are on) are kept in your browser's own extension storage. Removing the extension removes them.
  • Its panel loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. That request asks for fonts, not for your text, and without it Sophia uses your system's fonts.

Sophia for Mac (in development)

  • Grammar is checked on your Mac, and rewrites use Apple's on-device model. The app contains no networking code at all.
  • It uses Accessibility permission to read the text field you're in and to apply a fix you accept. Secure fields and password managers are skipped.
  • Your personal dictionary and settings are files on your Mac in ~/.config/sophia.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics, trackers, forms or third-party scripts. The page's security policy blocks them outright.
  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and downloads come from GitHub. Like any web host, both handle standard request data (such as your IP address) to serve and protect them.
